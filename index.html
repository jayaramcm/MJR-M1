    Where is MJR Township?

    At Survey No. 720 & 729, Tirupati Highway (NH 167K), Kalwakurthy, Nagarkurnool district, Telangana.

    How many plots are there, and what sizes?

    There are 104 residential plots across 7.93 acres, ranging from approximately 120 to 550 sq. yd.

    Is it a gated community?

    Yes. MJR Township is a gated community with 30 ft and 40 ft internal roads, and a 150 ft wide abutting road.

    Is the project RERA registered?

    Yes. The TG RERA number is P01600010465.

    What amenities are planned?

    A swimming pool, children’s play area, senior citizen park, multipurpose lawn, outdoor fitness area, three additional parks with pergolas and sit-outs, space for meditation and yoga, dedicated water infrastructure, and a planned EV charging station.

    What is the price of a plot?

    Pricing depends on the plot you choose. Leave your number, and our team will call you with current pricing and payment details.

    Can I visit the site before deciding?

    Of course. Schedule a visit, and we will show you the plots and the layout in person.
