Location

WHERE CONNECTIVITY MEETS CONVENIENCE.

MJR Township sits on the Tirupati Highway (NH 167K) at Kalwakurthy, where the Srisailam Highway, the Jadcherla–Kodad Highway and the Hyderabad–Nagarjuna Sagar Highway bring the wider region within easy reach. Daily travel is simpler, and so is the way back to the city.
