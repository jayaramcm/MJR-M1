    The plan for 104 plots, 30 and 40 ft roads and three parks across 7.93 acres. Approval comes first: it’s what RERA registration is built on.

  2. DoneRegistered

    Registered with TG RERA

    Registration P01600010465 puts the project, its plan and its developer on record with the state regulator. Check it yourself.

  3. Coming upDate to be confirmed

    Roads, drains and streetlights

Location

On NH 167K, five minutes from Kalwakurthy town

  1. 01 / 03

    Right on the highway

    The gate opens onto the 150 ft Tirupati Highway (NH 167K), where the Srisailam, Jadcherla–Kodad and Hyderabad–Nagarjuna Sagar highways meet.

  2. 02 / 03

    Towards Hyderabad

    North along the Srisailam highway to the Outer Ring Road, Rajiv Gandhi International Airport, the Financial District and Gachibowli, with the proposed Regional Ring Road on the way.

  3. 03 / 03

    Everything else, close by

    Schools, colleges, hospitals and temples in Kalwakurthy and Nagarkurnool, with Jadcherla to the west and Srisailam to the south-east.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

01What is the price of a plot?

Prices depend on the plot's size, road and facing. Ask on WhatsApp and we'll send the current price list.

02Is the project RERA registered?

Yes. MJR Township is registered with TG RERA under number P01600010465. You can check it on the TG RERA website.

03How many plots, and what sizes?

104 residential plots on 7.93 acres, from about 120 to 550 sq. yd.

04Is it a gated community?

Yes. It's a gated layout with 30 and 40 ft internal roads and frontage on the 150 ft Tirupati Highway.

05Where exactly is the site?

Survey No. 720 & 729, Tirupati Highway (NH 167K), Kalwakurthy, Nagarkurnool district, Telangana 509 324. Open in Google Maps.

06Can I visit before deciding?

Yes. and our team will show you the plots, the roads and the layout in person.
