# 05 · Content and copy

The words on this page sell a *feeling* ("foundation", "future", "vision"), but plot buyers decide on *facts* (₹, km, sq. yd., approvals). This file lists what to cut, what to rewrite and what to add.

## 1. Headlines are slogans, and most repeat one idea

| Section | Current headline | Problem | Rewrite (sentence case, factual) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero | A Foundation for Your Future. | Doesn't say plots, where or price | **Residential plots on the Tirupati Highway, Kalwakurthy** |
| Why MJR | Make Your Next Move Count. | Generic | **Four reasons buyers choose MJR Township** |
| Location | WHERE CONNECTIVITY MEETS CONVENIENCE. | Cliché, all caps | **__ min to Hyderabad's airport, 5 min to Kalwakurthy town** *(fill in confirmed drive time)* |
| Master plan | YOUR FUTURE ADDRESS | Doesn't say what to do here | **Choose your plot** |
| Amenities | A LITTLE MORE SPACE TO LIVE WELL. | Vague | **10 amenities inside the gate** |
| Amenities strip | MORE THAN YOU EXPECT. EXACTLY WHAT YOU NEED. | A second headline in one section | *Remove* |
| Trust | BUILT AROUND THE RIGHT FOUNDATION. | "Foundation" #2 | **Approvals, title and bank loans** |
| About | THE TEAM BEHIND THE FOUNDATION. | "Foundation" #3 | **MJR Infra Developers: 9 projects since 2012** |
| FAQ | FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS | Fine, but caps | **Questions buyers ask us** |
| Contact | A PLACE TO BUILD YOUR FUTURE. | "Future" again; not about contact | **Talk to our sales team** |
| Contact box | YOUR VISION DESERVES THE RIGHT FOUNDATION. | "Foundation" #4 | *Remove* |
| Form | LET'S FIND THE RIGHT PLOT FOR YOU | Caps | **Get a call back in 30 minutes** *(if true)* |

"Foundation" or "future" is the headline idea in **8 of 12** headlines. Readers stop noticing it after the second time.

**Rules:** sentence case (no ALL CAPS headings); a number or a place name in every headline; at most 8 words.

## 4. Wording fixes

| Current | Fix | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Actual entrance render" | "Entrance: architectural render" | "Actual" and "render" contradict each other |
| "Click any plot number…" | "Tap a plot number…" (or "Select") | Most users are on touchscreens |
| "Sold Out" (a single plot) | "Sold" | "Sold out" means the whole project |
| "Reserved / EV" | "Not for sale" | Users don't know what "EV" means in a legend |
| "Get Matching Plots" | "Send me matching plots on WhatsApp" | Says what will happen |
| "Request a Callback" | "Call me back" | Shorter, active |
| "Download Brochure" (no download happens) | "Get the brochure on WhatsApp" | Say what really happens |
| "Since 2012 / 12+ years of experience" | Keep one | Duplicate |
| "Plot #45" vs "Plot 45" vs "Plot 45," | "Plot 45" everywhere | Consistency |
| "30 & 40 ft" vs "30 & 40 FT" vs "30 and 40 feet" | "30 & 40 ft" everywhere | Consistency |
| "Interactive Master Layout Plan (104 Plots) · Availability as of…" | "Availability updated 1 Nov 2026" | Shorter; dated from the data |

## 5. Language

- Add **Telugu** (తెలుగు) for at least: the hero, the location distances, the FAQ, the form labels and the CTAs. Put a simple **EN | తె** switch in the header.
- Mark the brand and RERA number `translate="no"` so automatic translation doesn't garble them.
- The minimum body size should be **16px** and labels **14px**, for older family members reading on phones.

## 6. CTA vocabulary (use only these)

| Intent | Label | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Fastest contact | **WhatsApp us** | wa.me deep link with a pre-filled message |
| Voice | **Call** | tel: |
| High intent | **Book a site visit** | Modal with date and time |
| Comparing projects | **Get price list** | WhatsApp or form → PDF |
| Specific plot | **Ask about Plot 45** | WhatsApp with the plot number pre-filled |

"Book a Site Visit" appears **9 times** today. It should appear at most 3 times: header, hero or plot panel, and contact.
